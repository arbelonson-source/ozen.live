Cookies

Last updated 13 October 2026.

This website uses no cookies. That's why you don't see a cookie banner here.

What this site stores on your device

Nothing. Besides cookies, the site also doesn't use local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, service workers, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or anything similar. Even the animation's pause button doesn't remember your choice between visits, so that nothing is stored.

Our host

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When we checked on 13 October 2026, GitHub set no cookies on this site. If you follow a link to GitHub, TikTok or another site, that site's own cookies and policies apply.

The Ozen app

The app doesn't use cookies or advertising identifiers, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

If this ever changes

We'd update this page first, and ask for your consent before anything was stored on your device.
