About Ozen

Ozen is a free, open-source app that writes what people around you say as captions on your phone. It's made by an independent developer in Israel. It isn't offered by, or connected to, any government body, hospital or company.

Ozen is still in early testing and isn't in the app stores yet. It may change, or stop being available, at any time. By using this website or the app, you agree to these terms.

Safety: please read this

Not a medical device

Ozen is not a medical device and not a hearing aid. It doesn't diagnose, treat, measure or monitor any condition, and it doesn't replace care from a doctor, an audiologist or another professional.

Captions can be wrong

Speech recognition makes mistakes, especially with background noise, accents, several people talking at once, names, numbers, and medical, legal or technical words. Don't rely on Ozen when a missed or wrong word could cause harm. If it matters, ask the person to repeat it or write it down.

Not a certified alarm

Ozen's alerts for your name, a doorbell, a smoke alarm or a siren can be late, can be missed, and can be wrong. They depend on what the phone's microphone can hear, and on its settings, battery and volume. Never use Ozen as your only way to know about a fire, smoke, gas, a siren or any emergency.

Keep the official alerts on

  • In Israel, install the official Home Front Command app, turn on its alert option for people who are deaf or hard of hearing, and keep the phone's emergency alerts turned on. Official site: oref.org.il.
  • Use certified smoke alarms with a flashing light or a vibrating alert, made for people who are deaf or hard of hearing.
