Ozen

Live captions for the people around you.

Ozen turns what people say into large captions on your phone, as they speak. Made for people who are deaf or hard of hearing. Free and open source.

See Ozen on GitHub

The captions are written on the phone itself, with no internet after a one-time download. No account, no ads, no tracking. A new website is on its way.
